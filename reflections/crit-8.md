# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

For a long time I could not choose a project. I wanted one idea that would
get a good mark and also be useful to me, so I kept switching. What moved
things was a small change in how I looked at my own investment. I hold SPMO,
and I wanted to "improve the strategy". But I can't change which stocks the
fund buys. I only control how much I hold and when. After I saw that, the
project was clear: a room for testing rules on top of a momentum portfolio.
It also gave a reason for many people to share one room. Every extra test
makes a lucky result more likely, so the room should count all the tests.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I trade with real money, so I already worry about fooling myself with a
backtest. This week I found the same problem in my software. My own checks
put 10 fake trials into the room, and my own rule said trials could never be
deleted. Being more careful next time would not fix that. I wrote the rule
down where the agent will read it. I want to build tools that make it hard
to fool yourself. When I make a mistake, I want to fix it by changing the
system, and not only by remembering it.
