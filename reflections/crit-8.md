# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

For a long time I could not choose a project. I wanted one idea to give a good
mark and also be useful to me, and I kept switching. The breakthrough was a
small change in how I saw my own investment. I hold SPMO, and I wanted to
"improve the strategy". But I cannot change which stocks the fund buys. The
only things I control are how much I hold and when. Once I saw that, the
project became clear: a room for testing rules on top of a momentum portfolio.
It also gave me a reason for many people to share one room. Every extra test
makes a lucky result more likely, so the room should count all of them.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I trade with real money, so I already worry about fooling myself with a
backtest. This week I saw that the same problem exists in software. My own
checks put 10 fake trials into the room, and my own rule said they could never
be deleted. The fix was not just to be more careful next time. It was to write
the rule down where the agent will read it. I want to be a developer who builds
tools that make it hard to fool yourself, and who fixes a mistake by changing
the system, not only by remembering it.
