How a test works

Each month, the portfolio is reset to the chosen multiple of your money. Borrowed money costs the one-month US Treasury bill rate plus 1.5% a year. If the money ever runs out, it stays at zero. The portfolio is the top 10% of US stocks by their return over the previous year (skipping the last month), weighted by company size. It is similar in spirit to a momentum fund, but it is not any real fund.
