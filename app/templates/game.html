Capture the flag

Two teams on a small grid. Step on the other team's flag, then carry it back to your base to score. On your own half you catch anyone from the other team you touch; on theirs, they catch you. Empty seats are played by bots, so you can play alone with a bot teammate, or with friends: anyone who opens this page joins the same game.
