Which bot is the better teammate?

A good bot teammate plays for its team (the README, point 4). This page puts two kinds of evidence side by side: a large test of bots beside other bots, and the small count of what happened beside people here.

"Team captures" counts every capture by the teams that had this bot as a teammate: how many were scored by people and how many by bots. It shows whether a bot helped people score or scored on its own. Matches are not independent samples: one person may play many of them, and some matches are short and casual. A capture made while a bot covered for someone who left counts as a bot's. Only anonymous labels are kept, never cookies.
