How to read this

"Timing added per year" is what a rule earned beyond holding more or less of the portfolio, in the locked years: the part of its monthly returns that the baseline can't explain, times twelve. "Always" rules only scale the portfolio, so they have no timing. The chance the timing helps is the figure the room saw when the round closed.

The locked years are roughly the years SPMO has existed. The portfolio is a momentum portfolio, not SPMO, and the costs are rough allowances. This is history, not advice.
